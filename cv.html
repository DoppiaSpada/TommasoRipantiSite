ABOUT ME

I hold a Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering (L-8) from the University of Bologna (graduated with 109/110) and I am currently in the 2nd year of my Master's degree in Cinema and Digital Media Engineering (LM-32) at Politecnico di Torino. My profile stands at the intersection between robust software engineering and creative digital media, developed across seven interconnected professional domains:

As a Software Programmer & Engineer, I write high-performance, maintainable code in C, C++, Java, Python, and JavaScript across environments such as Node.js, Apache Tomcat, and FastAPI. In enterprise and networking projects (WMS for Unibo Motorsport, Healthy Helper, PicturesExtractor), I applied core hard skills in data structures, relational databases, RESTful APIs, and multithreading, backed by soft skills in rigorous debugging, analytical problem solving, and software reliability.

As a Software Designer, I architect modular systems following the RUP methodology and UML modeling. In my Bachelor's thesis (Spot Under Siege), I designed a distributed browser-based architecture comparing client-server and peer-to-peer WebRTC DataChannels with WebSocket signaling. In Spicy Mercante, I designed a hybrid MVP + Event-Driven model based on ScriptableObjects in Unity, exercising soft skills in systems thinking, architectural vision, and requirement analysis.

As a Game Designer, I translate psychological and narrative concepts into compelling game mechanics. In Blurred Mind, I designed metaphor-driven platforming mechanics that evoke the fragmented memories of an elderly meteorologist suffering from Alzheimer's. In Chaos Between Us, I directed a narrative VR desktop experience depicting the search for a lost sibling, and in Game Thinking (Il Cortegiano), I formulated core engagement loops. My soft skills emphasize player empathy, creative storytelling, and iterative playtesting.

As a Game Programmer, I develop real-time gameplay, AI, and networking in Unity (C#), Unreal Engine 5, and Cocos Creator. My accomplishments include mouse-rotation mechanics, biome-dependent procedural NPC spawning, and market economies in Spicy Mercante, character swapping in Blurred Mind, and mobile arcade games on Android (Rebellion King, Jolly Cube, Tap Bird), grounded in agile collaboration in student game teams (Level Up Lab).

As a Video and Audio Editor, I master complete production pipelines from shooting with RED cinema cameras, Insta360 360° cameras, and boom/lavalier microphones to post-production in DaVinci Resolve, Adobe After Effects, Reaper, and Pure Data. I directed spatial audio ambisonics and editing for immersive 360° VR documentaries (Risonanze at the Turin Conservatory, El Valentin) and produced the fantasy audiobook La Contea, drawing on soft skills in narrative pacing, acute auditory perception, and attention to sensory detail.

As an Animator and 3D / Technical Artist, I bridge technical constraints and artistic vision. In Blender, I executed full-character rigging, weight painting, pose-to-pose animation, and physics simulations for a commercial ad (CRAI 3D Animation). In Unreal Engine 5, I built end-to-end Technical Art pipelines: procedural environments with PCG and Linear Grammar, lighting and optimization with Lumen, MegaLights, Nanite, and RVT, MetaHuman creation with MoCap and Mesh-to-MetaHuman, photogrammetry via RealityScan, Gaussian splatting via Postshot, and custom Niagara VFX. Furthermore, I leverage Claude and Gemini AI workflows (instructions, context architectures, skills, connectors) to boost production velocity.
